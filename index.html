About Our Institution

Seven Decades of Spiritual Enlightenment & Educational Leadership

Founded in 1956 in Valanchery Moochikkal, Muhyissunna Secondary Madrasa has served as a center of authentic Islamic education for seven decades. Operating strictly under the guidance of the Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board (SKIMVB), our institution provides systematic Deeni instruction alongside strong moral formation.

Our curriculum covers Holy Quran Recitation with Thajweed, Fiqh (Jurisprudence), Aqeedah (Theology), Islamic History (Thareekh), and Arabic Language. We nurture upright, knowledgeable, and socially responsible individuals grounded in noble Islamic traditions.

Our Vision

To be an exemplary center of authentic Islamic learning and moral leadership.

Our Mission

Delivering structured Samastha curriculum with character-building Deeni values.

Curriculum & Classes

Comprehensive SKIMVB Syllabus (Classes 1 to 10)

Our Madrasa provides structured Deeni education strictly from Standard 1 to Standard 10 according to the prescribed Samastha syllabus.

Core Religious Subjects

Systematic instruction based on authoritative SKIMVB textbooks covering Thajweed, Fiqh, Aqeedah, Akhlaq, Thareekh, and Arabic language.

  • Quran Recitation with Correct Rules
  • Islamic Jurisprudence & Practices
  • Moral Values & Prophet's Biography

Classes 1 to 10 Structure

Progressive class levels structured from Primary (Classes 1 to 5) through Secondary (Classes 6 to 10) with regular evaluations.

  • Standard 1 to Standard 5 (Primary)
  • Standard 6 to Standard 10 (Secondary)
  • Board Public Examinations (5th, 7th, 10th)

Co-Curricular & Cultural

Fostering public speaking, leadership, and community engagement through student associations and literary festivals.

  • Meeladunnabi Celebrations & Festos
  • Quran Recitation & Speech Contests
  • Social & Moral Awareness Campaigns
